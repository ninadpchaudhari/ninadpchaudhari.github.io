PDF Templates

Download these, print them, or use them on a tablet.
Keep it simple: one sheet, one job.

1) Zero Draft Session Sheet

For when you’re stuck. Write first, edit later. Includes a 15-minute "entry ritual" and the "downhill slope" trick.

Download: Zero Draft Sheet (PDF)

2) Research Velocity & Streak Tracker

Track daily writing time, words, velocity (WPH), and whether you did “first thing” writing. Includes a weekly retrospective.

Download: Velocity Tracker (PDF)

3) Research Team Sync Log

A lightweight meeting log for Tu/Th (or any cadence): attendance, check-in, one main goal, blockers, and next meeting.

Download: Research Team Sync (PDF)

Instructor note (optional)

If you want the "why": these sheets are designed to reduce startup friction and turn writing into a repeatable routine. Nothing fancy... just consistent reps.

How to use these (quick)

  • Daily (When Stuck): do one Zero Draft session (15 minutes).
  • Daily: log your work in the Velocity Tracker (even 15 minutes counts).
  • Team meetings: start with the Team Sync log so you leave with one clear next step.

Tip: don’t aim for "perfect writing." Aim for written.
